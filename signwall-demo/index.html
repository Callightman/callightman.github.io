SignWall — App Review demo

Version 1.0, recorded on a physical iPhone with the built-in screen recorder.

  1. Local network permission request, shown and granted.
  2. The Apple TV app displaying its six-character pairing code, filmed on the television.
  3. The iPhone discovering that Apple TV over Bonjour and listing it.
  4. Entering the code and completing the pairing.
  5. Adding a photo slide from the phone's library.
  6. The slide being transferred and appearing full-screen on the television.

Two regions are blurred: the map inside the iOS permission dialog, and the contents of the photo library. Both contain the developer's personal information and neither is part of the app. Everything belonging to SignWall is unmodified.
