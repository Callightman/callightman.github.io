Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

IPTVflix does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server operated by the developer. Your information stays on your device.

What We Collect — Nothing

IPTVflix is a local media player. It does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, usage statistics, or any personal information.

What Stays On Your Device

  • Your playlist sources (M3U URLs or Xtream API credentials)
  • Your favorites, watch history, and playback position
  • Your language and playback preferences
  • EPG / TV guide data fetched from your configured source

All data is stored locally using SwiftData on your Apple TV. None of it is transmitted to IPTVflix servers — because there are no IPTVflix servers.

Credentials & Security

If you provide Xtream API credentials (server, username, password), they are stored securely in the device Keychain using Apple's standard security framework. They are never logged, transmitted, or shared.

Network Access

IPTVflix connects to the internet only to:

  • Fetch playlist data from URLs you provide
  • Stream media from URLs contained in your playlist
  • Fetch EPG / TV guide data from sources you configure

All network destinations are exclusively those you configure yourself. IPTVflix makes no other outbound connections.

Third-Party Services

IPTVflix does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or social login providers.

Children's Privacy

IPTVflix does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. Because no data is collected, this app is safe for use by all ages from a data-privacy perspective.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly.

Support

Getting Started

How do I add a playlist?

Open Settings in the tab bar, then tap Add Playlist Source. You can import an M3U/M3U8 URL or connect via Xtream API using your server, username, and password.

Playback

How do I control playback?

Use the Siri Remote:

  • Swipe left/right — Seek backward/forward 10 seconds
  • Swipe up — Show audio, subtitle, and aspect ratio controls
  • Play/Pause button — Toggle playback
  • Menu button — Exit player or dismiss controls overlay
EPG

How do I set up the TV Guide?

In Settings, tap your playlist, then tap Add EPG / XMLTV URL. Enter your XMLTV source URL and tap Sync EPG Now. The TV Guide tab will then show program information for your channels.

Content

Why are there no channels after installing?

IPTVflix is a player — it does not include any built-in channels, subscriptions, or content. You must provide your own playlist URL from a service you subscribe to legally.
