Blinds for Apple TV

Controls during a tournament

  • Play/Pause: pause and resume. Select on the paused screen also resumes.
  • Right: next level (or the break, if one follows).
  • Left: restart the current level; press Left again within 3 seconds to go back one level.
  • Menu: pause; press again to be asked whether to exit.

No sound?

Open Settings inside Blinds and check that Chime, Voice and Ticks are on, then press Test sound. Also check the Apple TV's own volume and that the TV or receiver input is not muted.

Editing a structure

Every value is a stepper: focus the row and press left or right on the remote. "Fill…" builds a whole structure from a starting big blind. Payouts must add up to 100% before you can save. Built-in structures cannot be changed in place: long-press one on the home screen and choose Customise to make your own copy.

The screen turned off

Blinds keeps the screen awake while a tournament is running or paused. After ten minutes paused it returns to the start screen, and the Apple TV's normal sleep settings apply again.
