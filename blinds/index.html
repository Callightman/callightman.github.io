Blinds

Poker tournament blind clock for Apple TV · $4.99 once, no subscription

The level clock, the blinds and the next level on the TV where the whole table can see them. A casino bell and a short voice line on every level change and break. Four ready-made structures, or build your own with the remote.

What it does

  • Turbo, Standard, Deep stack and Home game structures — or your own: levels, antes, breaks, players and buy-in, prize payouts, registration cutoff.
  • One huge countdown, gold blinds line, "next" line, elapsed time and time to the next break.
  • Bell, voice ("Blinds up", "Break", "One minute", "Final level") and ticks, each with its own switch.
  • Pause, skip a level, restart a level — all from the Siri Remote. The screen never sleeps during a tournament.

Requirements

Apple TV HD or Apple TV 4K running tvOS 17 or later. No internet connection needed.
