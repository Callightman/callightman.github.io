Privacy Policy

Blinds · last updated 30 September 2026

Blinds collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no network connection and no server operated by us.

What we collect

Nothing. Blinds has no sign-up, no login and no telemetry of any kind.

What stays on your Apple TV

Your saved tournament structures and settings are stored on the Apple TV itself and are removed when you delete the app. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

None. Blinds contains no third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising. The bell and voice lines are bundled in the app.

Children

Blinds is a poker tournament clock and is not directed at children. Since it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
