Rounds for Apple TV

Controls during a workout

  • Play/Pause or click the touch surface: pause and resume.
  • Left / Right: previous or next interval.
  • Menu: pause; press again to exit the workout.

No sound?

Open Settings inside Rounds and check that Voice cues, Beeps and Music are on, then press Test sound. Also check the Apple TV's own volume and that the TV or receiver input is not muted.

Editing a workout

Every value is a stepper: focus the row and press left or right on the remote. Presets cannot be changed in place — press Save as new to keep your version under My workouts. Long-press a saved workout to edit, duplicate or delete it.

The screen turned off

Rounds keeps the screen awake while a workout is running or paused. After ten minutes paused it returns to the start screen, and the Apple TV's normal sleep settings apply again.
