Privacy Policy

Rounds · last updated 24 September 2026

Rounds collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no network connection and no server operated by us.

What we collect

Nothing. Rounds has no sign-up, no login and no telemetry of any kind.

What stays on your Apple TV

Your saved workouts and settings are stored on the Apple TV itself and are removed when you delete the app. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

None. Rounds contains no third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising. The music and voice cues are bundled in the app.

Children

Rounds is a fitness timer and is not directed at children. Since it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
