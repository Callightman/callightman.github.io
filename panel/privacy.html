PANEL — COMIC & MANGA READER FOR APPLE TV

Privacy Policy

Panel collects nothing.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you read is recorded anywhere outside your own devices and your own servers.

Network

The app makes network connections only to the servers you configure yourself — your Komga instance or your SMB share — and only to list your comics, load pages, and, where the server supports it, store your reading position on that server.

What is stored, and where

Your server address, username and password are stored on your Apple TV. Passwords are held in the system keychain. None of it is sent to us, because there is no “us” to send it to: no server of ours is involved at any point.

Reading positions and cached cover images are stored on your Apple TV and are removed when you remove the app.

Children

The app displays the files you provide and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
