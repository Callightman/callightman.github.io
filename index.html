Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my IPTV playlist?

Open the app and go to Settings → Add Playlist. You can import via M3U URL or Xtream Codes API (server URL, username, password).

My streams are not loading. What should I do?

Check that your IPTV provider subscription is active and that your Apple TV has a stable internet connection. Some providers require specific stream formats — contact your IPTV provider if the issue persists.

Are my credentials stored securely?

Yes. All Xtream API credentials (server, username, password) are stored exclusively in the Apple Keychain on your device. They are never transmitted to any third party or logged anywhere.

Does IPTVflix provide IPTV content or channels?

No. IPTVflix is a player application only. You must have your own IPTV subscription from a provider of your choice. IPTVflix does not supply, host, or recommend any content or channels.

Which playlist formats are supported?

IPTVflix supports M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes API. XMLTV EPG (Electronic Programme Guide) is also supported for compatible providers.

App Information

IPTVflix requires tvOS 17.0 or later and an active IPTV subscription.
